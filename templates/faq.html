Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about records, verification, privacy, and support in HashVera.

<Back

What can I verify with HashVera?

You can verify academic records using a certificate ID or block hash. The verification page checks whether the record exists and whether the blockchain ledger remains valid.

Who can add or edit academic records?

Authorized roles such as administrators, faculty, and exam cell users can manage records based on the permissions configured by the institution.

Can a blockchain record be deleted?

Active records can be archived or corrected according to role permissions. Corrections should preserve an audit trail so verification history stays transparent.

Is private student information public?

No. Public verification is designed to confirm record authenticity without exposing unnecessary private profile details.

What should I do if something looks wrong?

Use the support page to contact the institution team. Students can also request record corrections where the workflow is available.
