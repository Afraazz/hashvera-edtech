Privacy Policy

How we protect and manage your academic data on the HashVera network.

<Back

1. Data Collection

We collect only the necessary academic information (Name, Student ID, Course, Marks) provided by your institution to secure your records on the blockchain.

2. Blockchain & Encryption

Your sensitive data is encrypted before being stored. Only the cryptographic hash of the record is permanently stored on the public blockchain ledger. This hash acts as a digital fingerprint that can verify your document without exposing your private information.

3. Data Access

Only you and authorized university officials can view the full details of your records. When you share a "Verification Link" with an employer, they are only granted access to the specific record associated with that link.

4. Your Rights

You have the right to view all your records and request corrections. Correction requests will be processed as new blocks on the chain, ensuring a transparent audit trail of all changes.
