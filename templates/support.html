Frequently Asked Questions

How do I verify a certificate?

You can use the 'Verify Certificate' link on the login page or scan the QR code on any official Digital ID issued by our network.

What if my record is incorrect?

Students can submit a 'Correction Request' directly from their dashboard. Our staff will review the request and mint a corrected block if approved.

Is my data public on the blockchain?

No. Only specific verification hashes are public. Detailed PII (Personally Identifiable Information) is encrypted and only accessible to authorized university personnel and yourself.
